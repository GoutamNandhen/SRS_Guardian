16. Known System Limitations

  1. Syntactic Scope: Rule-based and embedding analyses operate on syntax and lexicosemantic similarity. Deep domain logic completeness requires human engineering expertise.
  2. Language Support: Current NLP models and rules are calibrated for standard English requirements documentation.
  3. Standards Alignment: Checks evaluate machine-checkable syntactic characteristics based on ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148, but do not replace legal or regulatory certification.
  4. Human Authority: All findings are advisory and labeled "potential". The human reviewer possesses absolute authority to accept, edit, or reject any detection.
